02 / PROJECTS

Selected research & projects

Representative work drawn from research reports, project abstracts and presentations.

QUANTUM PHOTONICS01

Controlling Quantum Emitter Performance in Nanophotonic Cavities

Numerical study of a damped cavity strongly interacting with a quantum dot, with a telecom-wavelength silicon photonic-crystal nanobeam. The modelling framework connects FDTD-derived cavity parameters—quality factor, mode volume and resonance—to cavity-QED coupling, Purcell enhancement, emitter efficiency and weak/strong-coupling regimes.

FDTD · MEEP · Cavity QEDRead abstract ↓
QUANTUM OPTOMECHANICS / RL02

Reinforcement Learning for Gravity-influenced Entanglement Generation

Explores optimization of quantum-optomechanical experiment conditioning for gravity-mediated entanglement proposals. A continuous-action actor–critic approach based on Deep Deterministic Policy Gradient (DDPG) is applied to a simplified control problem, with policy performance compared against a planning algorithm with access to system dynamics.

DDPG · Control · Quantum sensingRead abstract ↓
HARDWARE SECURITY / IoMT03

Optical PUFs for Internet of Medical Things Security

Investigates physical-layer security for connected medical sensing. Optical physically unclonable functions are considered as a hardware security primitive to strengthen device authentication and tamper-resistance against cloning, invasive interrogation and model-building attacks.

Optical PUF · Biomedical devicesRead abstract ↓
PHOTONIC STRUCTURES04

Transmission Characteristics of Photonic Crystals

Transfer-matrix modelling of one-dimensional photonic band-gap structures for s- and p-polarized light. The study examines transmission spectra and how layer thickness influences stop-band centre wavelengths and bandwidths.

Transfer matrix · PBG · Optical communicationRead abstract ↓
SPINTRONICS / CIRCUIT MODELLING05

CMOS–MTJ and Spin-Transport Circuit Models

Compact and equivalent-circuit models connecting microscopic spin-transfer torque, spin-Hall effects, spin valves and magnetic tunnel junction behaviour with system-level circuit simulation. Work includes graphene spin transport and hybrid spintronic architectures.

PRELIMINARY RESEARCH REPORT · 2022

Theoretical Modeling of Quantum Light–Matter Interactions for Quantum Communications and Quantum Computing Applications

A simulation framework for on-chip quantum information processing. The report reviews cavity-engineered emission from quantum dots and nanodiamond color centres, proposes cavity geometries for controlled light–matter interaction, and uses three-dimensional FDTD to extract cavity quality factors and resonant frequencies for Purcell-enhancement optimization and quantum-photonic performance studies.

Project files / GitHub ↗
PROJECT REPORT · 2023

Reinforcement Learning for Gravity-influenced Entanglement Generation

Motivated by proposals in which gravitational interaction could entangle a spatially superposed atom with a mechanical oscillator, this project explores numerical experiment-conditioning and control. It adapts DDPG to a continuous-action domain and reports policies competitive with a planning algorithm that has full access to the dynamics and derivatives.

Project files / GitHub ↗
RESEARCH STUDY

Study of Transmission Characteristics in Photonic Crystals

Develops a transfer-matrix formulation for a one-dimensional photonic band-gap structure formed from two non-magnetic dielectric materials. It analyzes transmission modes and band gaps for both polarization states and reports shifts in stop-band centres and bandwidths as the denser-layer thickness increases.

Project files / GitHub ↗
PROJECT REPORT · IoT SECURITY

Hardware Security for Internet of Medical Things with Optical Physically Unclonable Function

Addresses device and data security in connected medical sensing, emphasizing hardware security and optical PUFs as physical authentication mechanisms to complement software-based cryptographic approaches in IoMT environments.
